The Isla Verde Land Registry raised its stamp fee from 0.4 to 0.5 percent of the declared price for every transfer registered from 1 July 2026. The registry's notice of 3 June gave buyers with signed contracts until 31 July to register at the old rate.

On a 180,000 CRC condo the increase is 180 CRC. On a 400,000 CRC villa lease it is 400 CRC. The [fees and taxes guide](/guides/fees-and-taxes-at-completion/) carries the new rate in its worked examples.

Buyers signing in July should confirm which rate the notary has applied on the completion statement; two statements seen by this desk in the first week still showed 0.4 percent.
