This card records what the developer published and has not been verified by an independent survey.

Marina Towers stands on the marina in [Playa Norte](/areas/playa-norte/), two eleven-storey blocks over a shared podium with a rooftop pool, handed over in November 2024. The developer's July 2026 price list shows 9 of 84 units still unsold, from 96,000 CRC for a 34 sqm studio facing the road to 340,000 CRC for a 118 sqm penthouse on the marina side.

The association was registered in March 2025 and set the 2026 service charge at 3.1 CRC per sqm a month. The statutes carry no minimum stay rule as of the August 2026 extract. Buyers should run the [title checks](/guides/title-checks-before-you-pay-a-deposit/) on the specific unit before paying a deposit.
