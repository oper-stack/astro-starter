This card records what the developer published and has not been verified by an independent survey.

Cerro Vista is a single five-storey block on the ridge road in [Cerro Alto](/areas/cerro-alto/), handed over in June 2022 and sold out by the developer in 2023. The August 2026 resale listings show three units offered, from 88,000 CRC for an 82 sqm two-bedroom on the second floor to 139,000 CRC for a 118 sqm three-bedroom with the two-coast view.

There is no pool and one lift. The approved 2026 association budget sets the charge at 1.9 CRC per sqm a month with a reserve fund of 14 percent of the annual budget, the strongest reserve position of the four buildings on this site's cards. Every unit is a freehold condominium title, as the [land ownership guide](/guides/land-ownership-rules-for-foreign-buyers/) describes for inland plots.
